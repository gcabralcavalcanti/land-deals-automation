# land-deals-automation

Cost x return model for a portfolio of land parcels. For each parcel it decides whether to subdivide it into lots
(loteamento) or resell it whole, projects every cost and receipt month by month, and computes VGV, NPV, IRR,
payback, margin and the maximum cash the business has to put in. It runs scenarios and a sensitivity analysis and
publishes the results as an Excel workbook, a PDF report with a one-page executive summary, and a static HTML
dashboard.

Claude is used where a formula cannot do the job: it turns a free-text description of a new parcel into structured
premises (which a person confirms), and it writes an investment memo per parcel in which every number is checked
against the model before the memo is kept.

**All data is synthetic, generated by `generate_data.py`.** Assumptions marked "illustrative" are typical values,
not market research.

![Dashboard](docs/dashboard.jpg)

## Run it

```
pip install -r requirements.txt
python -m land_deals demo
```

No API key needed: the demo replays the recorded AI answers. It writes:

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `reports/final-report.pdf` | executive summary, cash flow, parcels, NPV and IRR, scenarios and sensitivity, key assumptions, memos |
| `output/land_portfolio.xlsx` | Summary, Cash flow (quarterly and monthly), Parcels, Assumptions with sources, Costs, Sensitivity, Strategy, Memos |
| `output/dashboard.html` | the same charts and conclusions as the PDF; static, works on GitHub Pages |
| `reports/memos.md`, `reports/summary.json`, `reports/charts/*.png` | memos, headline numbers, charts |

Tests: `python -m pytest -q` (offline). Other commands:

```
python generate_data.py                                    # data/portfolio.json
python -m land_deals run [--no-memos]                      # model + outputs
python -m land_deals intake data/intake/gleba-12ha.txt     # free text -> premises, shown for confirmation
python -m land_deals intake data/intake/gleba-12ha.txt --accept   # adds the parcel to the portfolio
```

To run the AI live, put `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` in the environment or in `.env` (see `.env.example`).

## Results on the synthetic portfolio

| Portfolio | Base | Pessimistic | Optimistic |
|---|---|---|---|
| VGV | R$ 59.5 million (52.2 subdivided, 7.3 sold whole) | R$ 50.4 million | R$ 65.6 million |
| Net profit, margin on VGV | R$ 23.7 million, 39.8% | R$ 5.1 million, 10.1% | R$ 35.7 million, 54.3% |
| NPV at 14% a year | R$ 2.2 million | R$ -11.8 million | R$ 12.3 million |
| IRR per year | 16.6% | 2.8% | 31.5% |
| Maximum cash exposure | R$ 28.4 million (Sep 2028) | R$ 39.9 million | R$ 20.4 million |
| Payback (months after the first purchase) | 61 (Feb 2031) | 99 | 44 |

What the model says (the same sentences are generated into the PDF, the workbook and the dashboard by
`findings.py`):

- The 4 subdivisions carry the portfolio. The 6 parcels sold whole return 4.1% to 10.9% a year, all below the 14%
  hurdle, even bought 10% to 30% below market: holding land costs maintenance and tax while it appreciates more
  slowly than the hurdle rate.
- Infrastructure cost moves NPV the most: from R$ 5.9 million at R$ 80/m2 to R$ -4.0 million at R$ 160/m2.
- The cash need peaks at R$ 28.4 million and is recovered 61 months after the first purchase: the "return takes
  two to four years" of land development, in numbers.
- Subdividing beats selling whole in all 4 urban parcels where both are possible.

## The model

Ten fictitious parcels: three urban-expansion plots (glebas) and one urban plot big enough to subdivide, four
smaller urban areas and two rural small farms that are resold whole. Rule (a parameter): urban or urban-expansion
parcels of at least 20,000 m2 are subdivided; everything else is sold whole. The `Strategy` sheet shows both
options for every urban parcel, so the rule can be questioned.

| Stage | Costs and receipts |
|---|---|
| Acquisition | price, ITBI, deed and registry; CAR surveyor's fee for rural parcels |
| Holding | maintenance per m2 of unsold area (lower for rural land); IPTU (urban) or ITR (rural) on the land value, until sold |
| Subdivision | topography and approval projects over the approval period; streets, drainage, water, sewage and power plus site labor over the works period; fence and gatehouse; marketing |
| Sales | lots sold evenly over the absorption period (36 months base, 24 to 48), launched at approval; or the whole parcel after a holding period with land appreciation; broker fee; taxes on receipts (Lucro Presumido, cash basis) |
| Receipts | part cash, part financed: down payment plus Price-table installments with monthly interest, corrected once a year by a fixed IPCA assumption, net of a small default rate |

Every assumption sits in `land_deals/config.py` with a base value, a low and a high value, a unit and a source.
Public rules are cited: ITR rates (Lei 9.393/1996), presumed-profit taxes (Lei 9.249/1995, Lei 9.718/1998), IPTU on
urban-expansion areas (CTN art. 32), subdivision (Lei 6.766/1979), registry fees (state tables; SP Lei 11.331/2002).
Everything else is marked illustrative. The low and high values drive the scenarios and the sensitivity analysis,
which moves one assumption at a time. A parcel can override any assumption in `data/portfolio.json`.

**How the numbers are checked.** `tests/test_independent.py` rebuilds a subdivision, an urban whole sale and a
rural whole sale with numpy, straight from the description above, and compares every cost and receipt category,
the monthly cash flow, NPV, IRR (from the roots of the NPV polynomial), payback and maximum exposure. Other tests
check that installments repay the financed balance at the contract rate, that the correction steps once a year,
that sales span exactly the absorption period, and that the sensitivity moves one assumption at a time.

## Where the AI is used

| Step | What Claude does | What Python checks |
|---|---|---|
| Intake | reads a description such as "Gleba de 12 hectares ... deve fechar em R$ 3,8 milhões" and returns each premise as written, with the passage it came from (JSON Schema) | the passage exists in the text; units are converted by code (hectares, alqueires paulistas, "mil", "milhões"); missing premises get a default and are flagged; nothing enters the portfolio until a person runs `--accept` |
| Memo | writes a short memo per parcel (recommendation, thesis, risks, conditions) from the facts the model computed | every number in the memo must be one of those facts, written the same way; a negative NPV cannot be "invest". A memo that fails is retried once with the complaints, then dropped |

In the recorded run the intake picked the expected closing price (R$ 3.8 million), not the asking price
(R$ 4.2 million), and all 10 memos passed the number check on the first attempt.

## Cost controls

| Control | Setting |
|---|---|
| Model | `claude-haiku-5-5` (USD 0.10 / 0.50 per million input / output tokens), thinking disabled, effort low |
| Output budget | 900 tokens per intake or memo; a truncated answer is retried once with 1.5x |
| Call cap | `LAND_MAX_CALLS`, default 20 per run |
| Response cache | every accepted answer is saved in `recordings/` (versioned); an identical request is never paid twice, and without a key the recordings replay the run |
| Timeouts and retries | 60 s timeout, 2 SDK retries for network errors, 1 retry when the checks fail |
| Log | one JSON line per call in `logs/` (not versioned), with tokens and estimated cost |

The recorded answers cost about USD 0.004 (3 intakes and 10 memos). Building and revising this repository,
including an earlier document-extraction design that was replaced, used about USD 0.06 in total.

## Layout

```
land_deals/
  config.py      assumptions with ranges and sources, model and limits
  synthetic.py   the fictitious portfolio
  model.py       monthly cash flow per parcel, NPV, IRR, payback, exposure; portfolio, scenarios, sensitivity
  findings.py    plain-English conclusions shared by every output
  intake.py      AI intake: free text -> premises, unit conversion, validation
  memo.py        AI memo and its number check
  llm.py         API wrapper: recordings, call cap, retries, logging
  workbook.py    Excel output (openpyxl)
  charts.py      PNG charts, PDF report, HTML dashboard
data/            portfolio.json, intake examples
docs/            dashboard screenshot
recordings/      recorded LLM answers (versioned)
output/          workbook and dashboard
reports/         final-report.pdf, memos.md, summary.json, charts
tests/           pytest, no network
```

## Limitations and next steps

- Illustrative assumptions are typical values, not market research; the model is a decision aid to be fed with
  real quotes (infrastructure budget, lot prices from comparable launches, the company's hurdle rate).
- Sales are spread evenly over the absorption period; a launch peak and a real sales curve come next.
- The correction index is a fixed yearly rate, not the real IPCA or IGP-M series. Taxes use the presumed-profit
  regime only. Property tax is charged on the purchase price. Cancellations (distrato) are not modeled.
- Mixed strategies (subdivide part of a parcel and sell the rest whole) are not modeled yet.
